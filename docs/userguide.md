## Patron Guide

### Sign Up
  <h4 align="center">Click <b>Sign Up</b> button.</h4>
  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/blank_login.png" width="500"/>
  </p>
  <br>

<h4 align="center">Input email, password, password confirmation, first name, and last name. Click <b>Submit</b>.</h4>

  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/sign_up.png" width="500"/>
  </p>
  <br>

<h4 align="center">Return to the previous page, or click <b>Login</b> to log in.</h4>

  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/login.png" width="500"/>
  </p>
  <br>

### Home Page
<h4 align="center">After logging in, you will be greeted with this page.</h4>

  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/patron_home.png" width="1000"/>
  </p>
  <br>

<h4 align="center">Here, you can search for books using the provided search bar and an optional filter.</h4>

  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/patron_home_search.png" width="1000"/>
  </p>
  <br>

<h4 align="center">Click the <b>View Details</b> button under a search result to view more details, and <b>Reserve Now!</b> to reserve the title to check out later in person.</h4>

  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/patron_home_book_details.png" width="400"/>
  </p>
  <br>

<h4 align="center">On the home page navbar:</h4>
<br>
<h4 align="center">To view/cancel your reservations, click <b>My Reservations</b></h4>
  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/my_reservations.png" width="400"/>
  </p>
<br>
<h4 align="center">To view your checked out books, click <b>My Checkouts</b></h4>
  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/my_checkouts.png" width="400"/>
  </p>
<br>
<h4 align="center">To view, edit, or delete your account, click <b>My Account</b></h4>
  <p align="center">
    <img src="images/user_guide/my_account.png" width="400"/>
  </p>
<br>
